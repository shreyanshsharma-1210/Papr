## 1. Executive Summary

During the initial review, the storefront utilized external stock photo URLs with significant discrepancies (e.g., generic modern architecture photos for mechanical drafters, abstract art for rulers, glassware test tubes for chemistry manuals, generic trade books for school textbooks).

To achieve authentic, trustworthy retail quality, **all 29 catalogue products, 2 academic kit bundles, 7 category cards, and 6 Print Studio finishes** have been audited, photographically verified, and saved locally under `/public/images/`.

### Photographic Standards Implemented
- **True Physical Representation:** Every item represents the physical product sold. Casio FX-991CW displays the authentic Casio scientific calculator; spiral notebooks show twin-wire metal binding; drafting instruments display engineering mini-drafters.
- **Local Asset Hosting:** Images are permanently stored in `/public/images/` and served locally by Vite/Cloudflare at `/images/...`, eliminating external CDN rate limiting, CORS errors, and broken image containers.
- **Aspect Ratio & Frame Discipline:** Uniform `aspect-square` product containers with subtle neutral backgrounds (`#F7F5EF`), high-contrast Deep Plum hover borders, and crisp rendering.

## 6. Architecture & Delivery

```
public/
└── images/
    ├── products/     # 29 verified individual product photographs
    ├── kits/         # 2 verified flat-lay kit bundles
    ├── categories/   # 7 category cards
    └── print/        # 6 print studio service cards
```

- **Zero External Network Dependencies:** The entire catalogue loads offline and during local development without network lookups.
- **Vite Integration:** Automatically mapped to root `/images/...` paths in production builds.
- **Fail-Safe Fallback:** The `ProductImage` component in `src/components/ProductImage.tsx` automatically falls back to `/images/products/p-nb-200.jpg` if an unexpected image path fails.
